Understanding Vendor Assessment on GeM

Comprehensive breakdown of the mandatory verification milestones required for manufacturing brands.

Desktop Assessment Phase

Evaluators check your uploaded manufacturing proofs, trade licenses, utility bills, and quality testing reports online. Our experts pre-audit every file to prevent technical objections.

Video Assessment Phase

A live virtual inspection conducted by authorized agencies (such as QCI or NABCB) where your plant machinery, working staff, and raw materials are inspected via live video link.

Final Certification Report

Upon successful clearing of both desktop and video audit stages, your vendor assessment report is generated, unlocking unrestricted OEM status across all government bids.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why hire a GeM OEM registration and consultant in Lucknow?

Working with a local expert ensures fast-track paperwork processing, compliance with portal rules, zero errors in cataloging, and direct assistance for businesses across Uttar Pradesh and PAN India.

What documents are required for OEM panel creation?

You need a valid Trademark certificate (or application receipt), GST registration, MSME/Udyam certificate, factory license or MSME databank registration, and authorized signatory proofs.

Can secondary sellers list my brand without my approval?

Once your OEM panel is established, no third party can list your brand items without explicit authorization tokens generated through your master dashboard, giving you total pricing control.

What is vendor assessment on GeM and how do desktop/video steps work?

Vendor assessment verifies the physical existence and capabilities of manufacturing units via desktop document audits and live video inspections by agencies like QCI, which is mandatory for specific product categories.
