Frequently Asked Questions About GeM Registration

Clear answers to common queries regarding vendor onboarding, compliance, and bidding.

1. What is GeM Portal Registration and who needs it?

Government e-Marketplace (GeM) is the national public procurement portal for central and state government departments. Any manufacturer, trader, or service provider wanting to supply goods or services to government bodies must complete GeM vendor registration.

2. Is GST registration mandatory for GeM portal signup?

Yes, for most commercial sellers and business entities, a valid GSTIN is mandatory. However, certain specific categories like artisans or weavers registered under specific handicraft boards may have different provisions, but general businesses require GST.

3. How long does it take to complete GeM vendor onboarding?

With expert consultancy under Karan Yadav, basic primary profile registration is completed quickly. Total time depends on OEM brand approval and catalog verification, typically taking between 2 to 5 working days.

4. What are the benefits of Udyam/MSME integration on GeM?

Linking your MSME or Udyam registration helps you claim exemption from Earnest Money Deposit (EMD), security deposits, and lets you access special price preference criteria during government tender evaluations.
