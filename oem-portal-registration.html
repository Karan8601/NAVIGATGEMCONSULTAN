Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Why hire a GeM OEM registration and consultant in Lucknow?

What documents are required for OEM panel creation?

You need a valid Trademark certificate (or application receipt), GST registration, MSME/Udyam certificate, factory license or MSME databank registration, and authorized signatory proofs.

Can secondary sellers list my brand without my approval?

Once your OEM panel is established, no third party can list your brand items without explicit authorization tokens generated through your master dashboard, giving you total pricing control.
